How we work together

Reach out with your wedding date, chosen destination or venue, and overall celebration vision. We review our calendar and reply within 24 hours regarding date availability.

We connect over a private video call or in our Paris / New York studio. We talk through your event flow, lighting timelines, and the personal nuances that matter most to you both.

Secure your wedding on our calendar with a straightforward digital agreement and a 50% retainer. Once signed, your dates are locked exclusively, and we turn away all overlapping inquiries.

A relaxed pre-wedding shoot that feels like an evening stroll. This allows us to establish complete comfort in front of the lens so that by your wedding morning, having cameras around feels entirely natural.

You celebrate fully and stay immersed in the moment. We document quietly, capturing tears, laughter, stolen glances, and vows without asking you to pose or reenact a single sacred moment.

Receive a curated highlight teaser within 72 hours. Your complete color-graded high-resolution gallery and 4K films are delivered in 6–8 weeks, accompanied by bespoke layout proofs for your handcrafted Italian leather albums.

Frequently asked questions

Our wedding collections include comprehensive full-day coverage (typically 10 to 14 hours per day). We do not count hours or rush moments; our crew arrives during morning preparations and stays through to the final celebrations.

Yes! More than 75% of our calendar comprises destination events across Lake Como, Amalfi Coast, Provence, Cotswolds, and Paris, as well as international destinations including New York, Aspen, and Bali. We coordinate travel logistics directly with your planners.

For a multi-day celebration, we deliver between 800 and 1,500 meticulously edited, high-resolution digital photographs. Every delivered image is color-corrected and ready for large-format fine-art printing.

A 50-photo teaser and social media film teaser are delivered within 48 to 72 hours of your wedding. Full color-corrected online galleries arrive in 4–6 weeks, and full 4K master films in 6–8 weeks.

We provide comprehensive uncut documentary ceremony edits (full vows, speeches, first dances). Unedited RAW camera files can also be provided on an external rugged SSD upon request.

A 50% retainer secures your date on our calendar. 40% is due one month before the wedding day, and the final 10% is settled upon delivery of your teaser trailer and online gallery preview.

All collections feature a dedicated multi-person crew (typically 4 to 6 specialists) including lead cinematographer Julian Vance, dedicated fine-art photographers for each partner, and an aerial drone pilot.

Each album is custom-designed with your input and handcrafted with Italian leather or fine linen covers. Pages are seamless flushmount spreads printed on heavy archival cotton paper guaranteed not to fade for over a century.

Every camera records to dual memory cards simultaneously. At the end of every shooting day, all media is cloned to two mirrored SSDs on-site, plus redundant cloud backup. We carry complete global public liability and equipment insurance.

If unexpected circumstances require shifting your dates, your deposit transfers directly to any mutually open date within 12 months with no penalty fees or hidden charges.
